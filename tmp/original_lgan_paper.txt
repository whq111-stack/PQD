         Abstract—Multiple power quality disturbance (PQD) con-
      tains various single disturbances, so its classification is
      essentially a multilabel classification task. Due to the
      complexity of multilabel tasks, the performance of exist-
      ing multilabel methods for PQD is hard to meet practical
      needs, which severely restricts the engineering applica-
      tion of multilabel methods. Therefore, in this article, we
      propose a novel multilabel method named LGAN, which
      incorporates deep learning and explores the correlations
      between PQD labels to improve performance. First, 1-D con-
      volutional neural network extracts features automatically
      from PQD signals. Then, a label-guided attention module                         Fig. 1. Framework contrast between the proposed method and the
